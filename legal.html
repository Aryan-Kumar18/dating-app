Terms & Privacy

Terms of use

  • Who can join: only people aged 18 or older who are IIT Madras BS students, plus testers an admin has approved. If you enter an age under 18, your email is blocked from using the app.
  • Be respectful: no harassment, fake profiles, spam, hate or illegal content. Use the report and block buttons if someone behaves badly.
  • Moderation: an account reported by 10 different people is suspended automatically. Admins can suspend or remove accounts and only admins can lift a suspension.
  • Premium: Premium costs ₹50 for 30 days and is activated after a successful payment. For payment problems, use Contact us in the app.
  • No guarantees: this is a student-run service provided as is. We cannot promise matches, availability or that other users are who they say they are. Meet people safely.

Privacy

  • What we store: your student email, name, age, cohort, level, bio, interests, photo, likes, matches, messages, reports and payment references.
  • Who sees it: other users see your profile unless you hide it. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. Admins can review reported conversations and, when needed for safety, other conversations.
  • Services we use: Supabase (database and login), Google (sign-in), Razorpay (payments) and Web3Forms (contact messages).
  • Your control: you can hide your profile, or delete it from the Me tab. A deleted profile disappears for other users immediately, and you can sign up again with the same email. A copy is kept in an admin-only archive for safety and report handling until an admin permanently deletes it. Payment records may be kept as needed for accounts and legal reasons.
  • We do not sell your data.

Questions? Use Contact us inside the app.
